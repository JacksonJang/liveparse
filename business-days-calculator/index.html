“Business day” is not a universal weekday

Weekend and holiday inputs create the calculation calendar

A Monday-through-Friday week is common, but it is not universal. Some organizations work six days; others use Friday and Saturday as their weekend; a shift schedule may not map cleanly to weekdays at all. This calculator excludes exactly the weekdays selected in the interface and makes that choice visible with the result.

Public holidays are equally contextual. A date may be a holiday in one country, province, city, exchange, school, bank, or company and an ordinary working day in another. Enter each relevant holiday as YYYY-MM-DD. Duplicate entries are treated as one date, out-of-range holidays do not change a range count, and a holiday already excluded as a weekend does not subtract twice.

An “observed” holiday is not inferred from the nominal holiday. If a Sunday holiday is observed on Monday under the governing calendar, enter the Monday date separately. Emergency closures and one-off company shutdowns also require explicit entries.

Eligibility and membership are separate tests

Endpoint inclusion never turns a non-working date into a working one

For a range calculation, first choose whether the start and end belong to the range. Each included endpoint must still pass the working-day test. Selecting “include start” does not make a Saturday count when Saturday is configured as weekend, and it does not override an entered holiday.

Working-calendar answers

Business days calculator FAQ

Which days are treated as weekends?

The selected weekend weekdays are excluded. Saturday and Sunday are a common default, but the visible setting is authoritative because working weeks differ across organizations and locations.

Does the calculator know public holidays automatically?

No. It does not assume a country, region, market, employer, or observed-holiday policy. Enter each holiday to exclude as YYYY-MM-DD and verify the list against the calendar that governs your task.

What if a holiday falls on a weekend?

That date is excluded once and is not double-counted. An observed weekday is a separate holiday only when you enter its own YYYY-MM-DD value.

Are the start and end dates included?

The selected endpoint options control whether an otherwise eligible start or end date counts. A weekend or listed holiday remains excluded even when its endpoint option is selected.

Can this result establish a filing or payment deadline?

No. Deadline rules may specify cutoff times, jurisdictions, emergency closures, observed holidays, service methods, and rollover behavior. Confirm the controlling rule and official calendar before relying on a date.
